# Surface: home (portfolio)

Scope: the single-page portfolio at `/` (src/App.jsx and its sections). Visitor mode: Experience. The Ubuntu simulator keeps its own authentic Ubuntu world and is out of this brief.

Audience and job: recruiters decide in under a minute; senior engineers look for depth; designers judge craft; curious visitors explore. Proof on hand: UTN Hub request diagram and captures, Eco Records photos, Gavia, live GitHub push.

Memorable moment: one copper trace that starts in the hero circuit and carries the visitor down the page, lighting a via at each section.

Unresolved: easter egg (phase 4), case studies (phase 5).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a working circuit board. One copper trace runs from the name to the contact pad; every small label is silkscreen carrying real data. It refuses the dark-violet developer template: Geist, glow edges, rounded cards, eyebrow labels.

OWN-WORLD: Matte board black #0d0c0a with warmer layers #14120f and #1b1814; copper #c97d4f, bright copper #e29a6c for active and signal; silkscreen text #ede8de, #b5ad9f, #928a7d. Archivo (Omnibus-Type, Buenos Aires) expanded and semibold for display, normal width for text; Chivo Mono only for data annotations. Hairline rules, pads with 2px radius, vias as rings, fiducial corner marks on images, copper-filled primary pads. No glows, no glass, no shadows as decoration.

STORY: The visitor reads who Vicente is and what he builds in the first viewport, sees UTN Hub working, scans the other projects, then experience, method, stack, the person, and ends on the contact pad.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, the name in Archivo expanded at display scale, role line and one-sentence pitch, copper primary "Ver proyectos", CV pad, quiet OS link. Right, the portrait framed by fiducials with silkscreen notes: Córdoba with live local time, last push. Copper traces across the ground, the spine starting bottom-left.

FORM: user-pinned direction "Placa de cobre" (no concept-seed roll; pinned directions beat the roll). Code-led: no image generation available.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
