# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Technical recruiters and hiring managers** deciding in under a minute whether to interview Vicente for a full-stack role (Argentina or abroad, remote or hybrid). They need role, stack, experience, location and a way to reach him without hunting.
- **Senior engineers** asked to review the candidate. They look for evidence of understanding: architecture, decisions, real deployments, readable code on GitHub.
- **Designers and creative people** who judge craft: typography, composition, motion, consistency.
- **Curious visitors** arriving from a link, who reward interaction, surprise and something to explore.
- **Potential clients of Gavia** (secondary): the portfolio backs the studio's credibility.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Vicente Aznar. Success means a recruiter understands who he is and contacts him, a senior engineer finds proof of depth, and a casual visitor remembers the site.

## Positioning

An engineering-minded full-stack developer who takes systems end to end across web, cloud and hardware: React/Django products deployed on his own Linux servers, plus physical builds (a Raspberry Pi record player with NFC, an ESP32 car). Co-founder of Gavia, a software studio in Córdoba. Few portfolios can show working hardware next to production web systems.

He studies Systems Engineering and AI at UTN and is not yet graduated: present "engineer" as a way of working, never as an earned title.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from LinkedIn, GitHub, the CV PDF, job applications and shared links, on desktop and mobile. Bilingual site (Spanish default, English toggle). Includes a hidden-in-plain-sight Ubuntu desktop simulator (games, terminal, apps) as an exploration space.

- Stack in daily use: React, Django, PostgreSQL, Linux, Nginx, Oracle Cloud; also Node/Express, Flask, Astro, TypeScript, React Native, C/C++ on Arduino/ESP32, Raspberry Pi.
- Work: Full Stack Developer at Porta Hnos (2025 to now); Globant internship (2023); co-founder of Gavia (2026 to now).

## Brand Commitments

- No "AI slop": no generic copy, no decoration without function, no unverifiable claims.
- No emojis in the interface.
- Keep the custom target cursor (owner's explicit choice), the intro name reveal, the Ubuntu simulator and the hero circuit background.
- Git: no Claude attribution in commits, PRs or branch names.
- The portfolio is the source of truth for experience and claims (the CV PDFs are older).

## Product Principles

1. Proof before claims: show the system working (diagrams, captures, live data) instead of adjectives.
2. A recruiter must never wait: content is readable immediately; delight is layered on top, never in the way.
3. Every effect carries information or responds to an action.
4. Personality through real material (hardware, Córdoba, the studio, Tolkien), never through costume.
